(What exactly does it do?)
SELENE is a web application designed to identify and compare potential landing sites on the Moon, especially near the South Pole. It analyzes sunlight availability, communication opportunities with Earth, and lunar terrain conditions.
Using interactive maps and 3D models, it helps users determine the most suitable locations and dates for future lunar missions, considering energy, communication, and safety.


(How does it work?)


Selene is a web application developed for the NASA Space Apps challenge 2026. Its main objective is to analyze and compare potential lunar landing sites in the moon, specially in the moon’s south pole region


The platform allows users to select different locations, set missions date and durations, and study the environmental conditions of each site, To achieve this, it combines astronomical and topographical data to evaluate two essential factors: solar energy availability and direct communication opportunities with earth 


Through its interactive tools, SELENE displays the lunar surface using 2D maps and 3D models. It also allows users to visualize the terrain, observe the positions of the sun and earth, and analyze how these conditions change over the time. Additionally, it includes comparisons features that helps evaluate different locations and identify which ones offer better conditions for a future mission


Selene uses data from NASA data from NASA sources such as JPL Horizons, LOLA, and LROC, which provide information about astronomical positions, lunar elevations, and surface images.
In conclusion, SELENE aims to support the planning of future lunar missions through a visual and interactive tool that helps identify and compare potential landing sites, considering important factors such as solar illumination, communication with Earth, and terrain characteristics.
